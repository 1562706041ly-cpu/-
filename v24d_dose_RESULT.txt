Observations (descriptive)
 1. gain = 0 is the original window (the VMD residual is kept), not a reconstruction. The v24m "reconstruction only" view is not a training view and is not a baseline for the perturbation.
 2. Signal change grows about linearly with gain up to 4 (0.045 -> 0.175, x3.9). The feature response is much steeper: r grows 12-fold between gain 1 and gain 4 (0.09 -> 1.12). At gain 4 the VMD perturbation changes the feature MORE than two independent standard views differ from each other (r > 1 in most models). Whether that helps is not known.
 3. At gain 4 the fidelity check begins to act (6.6 % of windows shrunk, none fall back); at gain 8 it acts on 58 % and the realised signal change saturates (0.234 vs nominal 2 x gain 4). Gain 8 is outside the pre-registered admissible range.
 4. Route changes (K >= 0.5 flips between x and x_g) grow from 4 % (gain 1) to 21 % (gain 4). The knowledge score K reacts strongly to the perturbation at gain 4.
 5. The 99th percentile LSD/tau is 0.95 at gain 4, close to the bound, for some windows.

Limits
 Warm-up models only (300 steps); after Chapter-3 training the encoder differs. Rk K only. Fixed-model dose scan: it shows that the dose works and where the check acts, nothing about H. 27 models are 9 splits x 3 seeds.
