v28 end-to-end exploratory screen of band-gain augmentation (independent vs coordinated): PRE-REGISTRATION DRAFT (awaiting the user's review; no training code has been written or run; nothing computed from this draft).
Bruinsma centrifugal pump only (Motor-2, 100 % speed). 75 % speed data sealed. Drafted 2026-11-06.

CONTEXT
 Chapter 3 leaves the VMD formulation (confirmed by the user). Evidence so far (all on these 9 splits, so not independent): no VMD variant beat Bg4 (v20, v23, v24, v24s2); v26 and v27 are mechanism checks on fixed warm-up encoders. v27 found that independent and coordinated band gains matched in signal level differ about 5 times in feature change (v27b scan, K/R budget). This screen asks one narrow question; it is not a confirmation and not a performance claim.

QUESTION
 With the K/R budget removed (uniform kappa = 1), does a band-gain second view (a) help over the standard augmentation alone (Bg4), and (b) does coordination across channels matter at matched intensity, either feature-level matched or signal-level matched?
 Removing K/R is a design choice to isolate the question. The earlier result was that allocation by (K, R) showed no benefit for the VMD budget (v24s2: Rk - Rks mean dH -0.001, interval containing 0); that is not a proof of no value, and it does not carry over to band gain automatically.

ARMS (all arms: the Bg4 pipeline = --mode generic --contrast plain --vmd-bank none --known-guard 0.4 --guard-group-key train_installation + the v22/v23 COMMON arguments; only the second view after warm-up differs)
  Bg4   existing seed-0 runs, reused (second view A(x)).
  BI65  independent band gain, gain 0.65 (selected by v28_gain_selection_RULE, see below).
  BC2   coordinated band gain, gain 2.0.
  BI2   independent band gain, gain 2.0.
 New runs: 3 arms x 9 splits (3 combos x 3 folds) x seed 0 = 27 (an earlier message of mine said 36; that count was wrong; with Bg4 reused it is 27).
 Second view after warm-up (step 300): A(band_view(x)), view 1 stays A(x). Both labelled and unlabelled windows receive it. kappa = 1 for every window.
 Band view: 32 linear bands (the partition of kr_modules.log_band_energy; DC in band 0, Nyquist in the last band); amplitude gain exp(0.5 u s_cb h) on every complex rfft bin of band b of channel c; u clipped to [-2, 2]; independent: u_cb independent; coordinated: u_b = the channel-1 draw of the same random tensor, shared by the four channels; budget s_cb = gain x mean over the known classes of the standard deviation across RECORDINGS (installation + measurement) of the record-mean log band energy of the labelled windows (window-level std if fewer than 2 recordings; the number of such classes is logged); fidelity: per-channel log-spectral distance <= tau_lsd (labelled windows, record key = installation, as in training), exponent halved up to 3 times, then fall back to x. The same random-number stream (seed + 20000) in all three arms.

GAIN SELECTION (design step; rule pushed before the scan: branch v28-rule, 2026-11-18T14:18:57Z; result in v28_gain_scan_RESULT.txt)
 Independent gain 0.65 minimises the mean |ln feature-change ratio| to the coordinated view at gain 2.0 on the selection half of the recordings (27 warm-up models, training windows only, no labels, no evaluation windows). The CHECK criterion (within a factor 1.25 on the held-out half of recordings in >= 23/27 models) is NOT met (15/27); the mismatch is concentrated in the three C3 splits (independent about 2 times weaker than coordinated there). Per the rule the gain stays 0.65 and the mismatch is a stated limitation. At this pair the coordinated view has about 2.5 times the signal change of BI65 (0.208 vs 0.082) and is shrunk in about 32 % of the windows (BI65: none).
 BI2 (independent 2.0) has a signal change within 20 % of BC2 (0.179 vs 0.208) and about 4 times its feature change (0.0154 vs 0.0036).
 DECISION NEEDED (see end): keep the constant 0.65 as is, or replace it by a label-free procedure at the end of each run's warm-up. This draft uses the constant.

COMPARISONS (H on the held-out installations, harmonic mean of known accuracy and unknown recall as in all earlier work; paired per split, seed 0; 95 % bootstrap over the 9 splits, 10000 resamples)
 Primary:   P1 BC2 - BI65 (feature-level matched, signal differs about 2.5x); P2 BC2 - BI2 (signal-level matched, feature differs about 4x).
 Secondary: S1 BI65 - Bg4; S2 BC2 - Bg4; S3 BI2 - Bg4.
 Descriptive (no rule): known accuracy, unknown recall, known-to-unknown rate, unknown cluster accuracy, NMI; unknown separability P1-AUROC only if the feature files of the runs are uploaded (labels for analysis only); the in-training feature change of each band view (below); per-split tables.

READING TABLE (fixed now; descriptive)
 BC2 ~ BI65 and both below Bg4: consistent with "the coordinated view looked better only because it perturbs less"; no support for coordination.
 BC2 > BI65 at similar feature change: coordination may matter, but the signal change differs about 2.5 times, so the effect of coordination is not isolated; P2 is read next to it.
 BC2 > BI2 and BC2 ~ BI65: at the same signal level, the larger feature change of BI2 may be what hurts; not separable from coordination.
 BI65 vs Bg4 alone: whether a weak independent band gain has any effect.
 No outcome here supports a causal sentence about coordination; a candidate would need a multi-seed stage.

DECISION RULES (exploratory screen; the same form as v24s2; the thresholds are proposals for the user to fix)
 CANDIDATE (for a later multi-seed stage): mean dH >= +0.02 AND positive in >= 7/9 splits AND the interval lower bound > 0, for a secondary comparison against Bg4 or a primary comparison.
 HARM: mean dH <= -0.02 AND the interval upper bound < 0.
 Otherwise: no evidence of a difference at this resolution.
 Resolution: the earlier single-seed stage-2 differences had a per-split standard deviation of 0.02-0.04, so the standard error of a 9-split mean is about 0.008-0.015; differences below about 0.02 cannot be separated from noise in this screen. Five comparisons, no multiplicity correction.
 If no comparison is a CANDIDATE: recorded as "no benefit observed in this setting"; the line "coordinated band gain improves performance" is stopped; thresholds are not changed, no arm is added, no further run is made on the same splits and seed to chase a result. If any comparison is a CANDIDATE, a multi-seed stage (seeds 1 and 2 of the same splits, which are not independent data) is pre-registered separately before it is run; the sealed 75 % speed data are not opened for selection.

VALIDITY AND RECORDS (fixed now)
 A run is invalid if the process fails, any printed loss is nan/inf, results_exp2.json is missing or a metric is NaN. Invalid runs are listed, not replaced, not re-run unless the failure is infrastructural with unchanged arguments; an arm with < 8 valid runs is not interpreted; with 8 valid paired splits the sign requirement is >= 7/8. No run is dropped or flagged because of its H. PowerShell logs are UTF-16 and are decoded as such by the analysis script (the v24s2 analyser bug).
 Pairing is verified by the analysis script: labelled indices, n_labeled / n_unlabeled / n_val identical to the reused Bg4 run of the same split; stored arguments equal to Bg4 except tag, the new flags and the gain.
 In training, every 300 steps (no random numbers consumed, no effect on training) each band arm logs, on the current batch: the median relative signal change of the band view, the shrink and fallback fractions, and the median feature change 1 - cos(enc(x), enc(v)) with the current encoder in eval mode (no gradient). These are implementation checks that the intended intervention was applied; they are reported per arm and per stage and are not outcomes. Feature matching at warm-up is not assumed to persist through training.

LIMITS (stated now)
 One seed per split: noise is not averaged; 9 splits from 3 combos share installations; the 100 % speed data were used for every earlier development decision, so this is not a confirmation; warm-up-based matching is imperfect (check criterion not met) and the encoder changes in training; band gain acts on magnitudes and is not VMD; sensor positions are unknown to me; the pump is a centrifugal pump, not a piston pump.

NOT CLAIMED: that coordination or any band gain improves H; that the signal- or feature-level matched pairs isolate the effect of coordination.
