Findings
 1. Under the existing fidelity rule the coordinated view cannot reach the feature change of the independent view at gain 2 (0.0111): it saturates near 0.0034 because the rule shrinks almost every window from gain 4 on. Per model, the needed coordinated gain was not reachable up to 12 in 9 of 9 models. Both arms saturate (independent near 0.016, coordinated near 0.0034).
 2. The reverse match is feasible: the independent gain whose feature change equals that of the coordinated view at gain 2 (about 0.0021) is 0.85 on average (per model 0.77-1.01).
 3. At that feature-matched pair (independent about 0.85, coordinated 2.0) the coordinated view has about twice the signal change (0.158 vs about 0.07) and the shrink fraction is small for both (about 0 and 0.04).
 4. Which of the two intensities is "the" matched level is a design choice that this note does not make. A match at the independent level would need a relaxed fidelity bound, which changes the constraint and would be a different experiment.
Limits: 9 models, one seed each, warm-up encoders; feature distance in a fixed feature space is not a measure of effect on H.
