CAVEATS (stated after seeing the result, not used to change any rule)
 1. B2 compares the MEDIAN signal change of the adaptive view with that of a view whose kappa is randomly permuted among unlabelled windows. Permuting keeps the kappa distribution, so the median barely moves by construction (observed 0.8 %). ALLOCATION-RELEVANT is therefore almost uninformative: 0/27 does not show that allocation is irrelevant. The window-level Spearman values above are the better descriptive evidence (the realised perturbation does follow kappa).
 2. r < 0.10 held in 16/27 models; the other 11 are between 0.10 and 0.148. The 0.10 cut was fixed in advance and is not changed.
 3. Warm-up models only (300 contrastive steps). After Chapter-3 training the encoder differs. The feature change is measured with an encoder that has just been trained to be invariant to the standard augmentation; small feature change is not by itself a defect.
 4. Same random numbers are used for V3 and V4 (torch seed), so d(V3,V4) isolates the VMD perturbation; V3' uses other numbers.
 5. K is only one of the quantities that react: route changes 4.2 %, which is far larger relative to the feature change (0.0012) than one would expect; the scorer is sensitive to tiny feature changes near the 0.5 decision boundary.
 6. Not tested: whether a larger budget (--kr2-gain) changes any downstream result.
